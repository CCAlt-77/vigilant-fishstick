    How to play

    You are the player at the bottom of the screen, hitting up the court. Your player runs to the ball on their own — your job is the shot.

    • •
      TapStandard drive into the open court.
    • ↑
      Swipe upDrive. How far up you drag sets how deep it lands — a short drag drops it near the service line, a long one pushes them back to the baseline.
    • ⇡
      Flick up fastThe same drive hit flat and hard.
    • ⇧
      Drag up past the topLob, high over their head.
    • ↓
      Swipe downDrop shot, dying just over the net.
    • ↔
      Swipe sidewaysAngled slice into the tramlines.

    The gauge

    While your thumb is down, a scale appears on the far side of the screen showing where your swipe sits: SHORT at the bottom, DEEP higher up, and a LOB band at the top. The shot it will play is named beside the marker, and the landing spot and the ball's flight are drawn on the court. Nothing is committed until you lift your thumb, so slide up and down until it says what you want.

    The sideways lean of any swipe steers the ball across the court — swipe up and to the right to drive down the right-hand line. Lean further for a sharper angle, but the closer you aim to the lines the more a mistimed contact will cost you.

    Timing

    A ring appears around your player when a ball is on the way. Swipe as it reaches you: meeting it while balanced gives a clean, accurate shot, while hitting on the run or at full stretch scatters it. Swipe early and it is buffered, so you can commit before the ball arrives.

    Coming forward

    Take a short ball inside the baseline and your player follows it in towards the net instead of trudging back, and volleys the next one out of the air. Good for finishing points — and the reason your opponent will start lobbing you.

    Serving

    The service box you have to hit is marked on the far court, and it swaps side every point. The target marker moves anywhere inside it: drag sideways to move it across, between the centre line and out wide, and drag further up to push it deeper into the box. How hard you flick sets the pace, separately from where you aimed, so taking care over placement does not cost you a big serve. Two serves, as usual — the second is automatically safer.

    Scoring

    Normal tennis. 15, 30, 40, deuce and advantage, six games to a set with a tiebreak at 6-6. Quick matches use a short set to four games.
