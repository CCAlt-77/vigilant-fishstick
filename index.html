How to play

  • The clues beside each row and above each column list the lengths of consecutive filled blocks, in order.
  • For example 4 2 means a run of 4 filled cells, then a gap, then a run of 2 — somewhere along that line.
  • Use logic to decide which cells are filled. Tap a cell in Fill mode to colour it. Switch to Mark mode (or right-click / long-press) to flag a cell as definitely empty.
  • Drag across cells to fill or mark several at once.
  • Every daily puzzle has exactly one solution and never needs a guess. Finish it to reveal the picture!
